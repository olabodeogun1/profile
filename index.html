I have spent ten years in Nigerian payments, the first six at United Bank for Africa, where I started in merchant acquiring and moved into technical product support. Since 2022 I have worked in product roles at PalmPay and Interswitch.

Most of my work has been onboarding merchants and billers and integrating with banks and other partners. I came into product management through software testing, user research and operations, so I am used to reading transaction data and system logs myself, and to writing requirements, user stories and wireframes that engineers can build from.
